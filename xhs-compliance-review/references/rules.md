# 小红书合规快审规则

This reference is for pre-publish screening of Xiaohongshu/Rednote copy. It is a practical guardrail, not an official exhaustive banned-word list.

## Output Categories

- 可以发: 信息分享、方法复盘、个人体验、公开信息整理, with no investment instruction or exaggerated claim.
- 修改后发: Contains soft risk that can be rewritten, softened, disclosed, or clarified.
- 禁止发: Contains hard financial risk, misleading claim, or platform-risk CTA that should not be published as written.

## Green Patterns

Safer finance-adjacent wording:
- “我用工具记录这次判断的依据”
- “这是一次研究过程复盘”
- “它帮助我把前提、证据和后续验证放在一起”
- “不构成投资建议”
- “仅分享我的研究流程”
- “不是买卖建议”

Preferred verbs:
- 记录
- 复盘
- 对照
- 验证
- 辅助
- 整理
- 追踪

Avoid verbs:
- 推荐
- 带你买
- 喊单
- 保证
- 承诺
- 预测收益
- 抄作业

## Manual Review Checklist

Ask for human review when:
- The post names a specific stock/company and makes a direction judgment.
- The post includes simulated or real-position screenshots.
- The post mentions returns, win rate, accuracy, or performance.
- The post includes product comparison or “AI analyst” capability claims.
- The author has a product/company relationship that may need disclosure.
- The post points readers to a link, group, DM, trial, registration, payment, or invite.
